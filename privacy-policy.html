Karteria Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Karteria ("the app") is an iPhone app with an Apple Watch companion, developed by Valerio Schips ("the developer", "we"). This policy explains what data the app uses, where it is kept, when (and only when) any of it reaches the developer or a third party, and what rights you have.

1. In short

  • Karteria has no server, no user account, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking SDK.
  • Your Health data is read and processed only on your iPhone and Apple Watch. The developer never receives it, except in the one case you start yourself: sending a data export to support (§7).
  • Two features use Apple services that contact Apple's servers: place names and maps (§5). Karteria does not sync your data with iCloud: the only copy outside your iPhone is your device's normal backup (iCloud or computer), if you have it turned on (§8).

2. Who is responsible

The developer, Valerio Schips (Italy), is the data controller for the personal data he receives directly: support emails and the data exports you choose to send him (§7). Contact: noserv_report@hotmail.com.

Data that stays on your devices (§3–§6) is processed by the app under your control. The developer has no access to it and cannot read, copy or delete it.

3. Data read from Apple Health (HealthKit), on iPhone

With your permission, given through Apple's system dialog, the iPhone app reads the following types from Apple Health. They are used only to compute and display the Overview, Runs, Compare and Sleep screens and their charts.

  • Workouts: your workouts (HKWorkout) with their statistics, events (pauses, resumes, automatic stops, segments) and metadata (indoor/outdoor, weather, elevation, time zone, recording device), plus the daily Activity ring summaries.
  • GPS routes of your workouts.
  • Heart and breathing: heart rate, resting heart rate, walking heart rate average, heart rate variability (SDNN), heart rate recovery, respiratory rate, blood oxygen saturation, VO2 max.
  • Running: running power, speed, stride length, ground contact time, vertical oscillation.
  • Walking and mobility: walking speed, step length, asymmetry, double support, walking steadiness, six-minute walk distance, stair ascent and descent speed.
  • Energy and activity: active and resting energy, exercise time, stand time and stand hours, physical effort, flights climbed, step count, distance (walking/running, cycling, swimming), swimming strokes.
  • Sleep: sleep stages (in bed, awake, core, deep, REM).
  • Other: environmental and headphone audio levels and exposure events, mindful sessions, time in daylight.
  • Personal characteristics: date of birth, biological sex, blood type, skin type (Fitzpatrick), cardio-fitness medication use. Date of birth is used to estimate heart-rate zones. The others are stored with your data but no calculation shown in the app uses them today.

When new data arrives in Apple Health (for example at the end of a run or of a night), the app may read it in the background to keep its charts up to date. The iPhone app does not modify or delete anything in Apple Health.

4. Apple Watch companion

During a run you start on the Watch, the companion:

  • reads heart rate, distance, energy, running metrics (speed, power, stride length, steps) and your date of birth (for heart-rate zones);
  • uses GPS location, only while the workout is running, to record the route and measure pace;
  • uses the barometric altimeter (Motion & Fitness permission), only while the workout is running, to show elevation gained. This value is shown live and is not saved;
  • when you end the run, writes to Apple Health: the workout, its GPS route, its events (pauses, resumes, segments) and technical metadata (segment numbers, a summary of GPS reception such as how many GPS points were received, and a flag if the run was recovered after an interruption);
  • if saving is interrupted (for example the system closes the app), keeps a recovery file on the Watch with the run's progress and GPS points. It uses that file to rebuild and save the run, then deletes it. When it rebuilds a run, it links to the workout the heart-rate, distance, energy, speed, power, stride and step samples the Watch had already recorded in Apple Health. This is why the Watch asks for write access to those types. It never creates new values.

Karteria does not use location outside an active workout on the Watch, and the iPhone app never asks for your current location.

iPhone ↔ Watch. The two apps exchange data directly between your own devices through Apple's WatchConnectivity: heart-rate reference values, the weekly training plan and the metrics you chose to show on the Watch (iPhone → Watch), and short spoken kilometre and interval announcements (Watch → iPhone). Nothing goes through the developer.

5. Apple services that contact Apple's servers

  • Place names (iPhone). To show a readable place name (for example "Milan") for a run, the app sends the run's starting coordinates to Apple's geocoding service. Only the coordinates are sent, with no other Health data. The result is kept only in the app's local database.
  • Maps (iPhone). Run routes are drawn on Apple Maps (MapKit), which downloads map tiles for the area shown.

Apple handles these requests under its own Privacy Policy. The developer receives nothing from them.

6. Data you enter in the app

Your shoes (name, purchase date, mileage values, the runs you assign to them), shoe photos, the weekly training plan and the app's settings. Photos are chosen through Apple's photo picker: the app receives only the photo you select and has no access to the rest of your library. All of this stays on your device.

8. Where data is stored, and how to delete it

  • Local database (iPhone). Health data read by the app is copied into a local database so charts load quickly. It is protected by iOS data protection and, like the rest of the app's data, is included in your device's normal backups (iCloud or computer), if you have them turned on: restoring a backup restores it too, and without a backup the app rebuilds it from Apple Health. Settings → "Full resync" deletes it and immediately rebuilds it from Apple Health. To delete it for good, uninstall the app (a copy may remain in backups made earlier, until iOS replaces or you delete them).
  • Shoes, photos, training plan, settings are included in your device's normal backups (iCloud or computer), if you have them turned on. Uninstalling the app deletes them from the device.
  • Watch recovery file: deleted automatically once the run is saved, or when you uninstall the Watch app.
  • Data written to Apple Health by the Watch (§4) belongs to Apple Health. You can view or delete it in the Health app.
  • Revoking access: in the Health app, your profile → Apps → Karteria, where you can turn off each data type. Location and Motion & Fitness permissions can be changed in Settings → Privacy & Security on the Watch.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR (articles 15–22) you can ask the developer for access to the data he holds about you (§7), for its rectification, erasure, restriction or portability, and you can object to its processing. You can withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect processing that already took place. Write to noserv_report@hotmail.com: you will get an answer within one month.

For data that never leaves your devices (§3–§6) you keep full control directly, through the settings in §8.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority: in Italy the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it), or the authority of the EU country where you live or work.

10. No automated decisions

Scores and indicators shown by the app (for example sleep score or training load) are informational estimates computed on your device. They produce no decision with legal or similarly significant effects (GDPR art. 22) and are not used for profiling.

11. Not a medical device

Karteria is not a medical device and does not provide diagnoses, medical advice or treatment recommendations. The data and metrics shown are for personal fitness tracking and information only. Talk to a doctor before any decision about your health.

12. Age

Karteria is not intended for people under 16, and the developer does not knowingly receive data from them. If you believe a person under 16 has sent data to the developer, write to the address below and it will be deleted.

13. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the "Last updated" date at the top changes. If a change affects how data is used or shared, it is also announced in the app's release notes before it takes effect.

14. Language

This policy is available in English and Italian. If the two versions differ, the Italian version prevails.
