Karteria ("the app") is developed by Valerio Schips ("the developer", "we"). This policy describes what data Karteria reads, how it is used, and how you can control it.

1. The principle behind Karteria

Karteria has no server of its own, no user account, and does not send your data to any external service. All data read from Apple Health stays exclusively on your device, in a local database created and managed by the app. The developer has no access to any of your data: there is no backend that receives it.

2. Data read from Apple Health (HealthKit)

With your permission, Karteria reads the following data types from Apple Health, used exclusively to calculate and display the Overview, Runs, Compare, and Sleep screens and their related charts:

  • Workouts: your recorded runs (HKWorkout), including statistics, events (pauses, resumes, splits) and metadata (indoor/outdoor workout, weather conditions, elevation, duration, time zone, device used).
  • GPS routes: the GPS track of your recorded runs.
  • Heart rate and cardio metrics: heart rate, heart rate recovery, heart rate variability (SDNN), resting heart rate, blood oxygen saturation, VO2Max.
  • Running metrics: running power, speed, stride length, ground contact time, vertical oscillation, cadence.
  • Walking metrics: speed, step length, average heart rate, asymmetry and double support percentage while walking.
  • Energy and activity: active and basal energy burned, exercise time, stand time, flights climbed, step count, walking steadiness.
  • Sleep: sleep stages (in bed, awake, light sleep, deep sleep, REM).
  • Other data: audio exposure (environmental/headphones), mindfulness sessions, time in daylight, distance covered (running, cycling, swimming, walking), swimming stroke count.
  • Personal characteristics: date of birth, biological sex, blood type, skin (Fitzpatrick) type, cardiometabolic medication use — used only when required by a specific calculation shown in the app.

Karteria reads this data; it does not modify or delete it in Apple Health.

3. Data written to Apple Health

The Apple Watch companion records your run live (heart rate, pace, GPS route) and, when you finish the workout, saves it to Apple Health as an HKWorkout — the same way Apple's own Workout app would. This write only happens for runs you actively record from the Watch, with your direct interaction (starting/stopping the workout).

4. Location

  • On the Watch, during an active workout, GPS location is used to record the run's route (saved to Apple Health, see §3). Karteria does not use location for any other purpose, and does not track it outside an active workout.
  • On iPhone, in the Compare screen, the app uses Apple's own geocoding service (not a service run by the developer) to translate the starting GPS coordinates of an already-recorded run into a readable place name (e.g. "Milan"). This request only uses coordinates already stored with the run: it does not request or read the device's current location, and does not save location anywhere outside the app's local database.
  • Maps shown in the app (run routes) use Apple MapKit, an Apple system service.

5. What we do NOT do

  • We do not collect, transmit, or sell your data to third parties.
  • We do not use your data for advertising, marketing, or profiling.
  • We do not use your data to train AI models.
  • We have no user account, so we do not collect email, name, or other identifying data through the app.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or tracking tools.
  • We do not sync your data to iCloud or any other cloud: it stays on the device where the app is installed.

6. Data retention and deletion

Data read from Apple Health is copied into a local database used by the app, so charts can be built quickly without querying Apple Health every time. You can delete this local copy at any time from the app's Settings screen. Deleting the app from your device also deletes this local copy.

The original data still lives in Apple Health, under your control via Apple's Health app: from there you can revoke Karteria's access to one or more data types at any time (Health → your profile → Apps and Devices → Karteria).

7. Medical device

Karteria is not a medical device and does not provide diagnoses, medical advice, or treatment recommendations. The data and metrics shown are for informational and personal fitness-tracking purposes only.

8. Children

Karteria is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a material way, we will update the date at the top of the page and, if the change affects how data is used, we will also note it in the app's release notes.
