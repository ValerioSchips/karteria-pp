Frequently asked questions

Does Karteria store my data anywhere outside my phone?
No. Karteria has no server or account: all data read from Apple Health stays in a local database on your device. See the privacy policy for details.

Why doesn't Karteria show my runs?
Check that the app has permission to read data from Apple Health (Health → your profile → Apps and Devices → Karteria) and that your runs are recorded as running workouts in Apple Health (from Karteria's Watch companion, Apple's own Workout app, or another compatible app).

How do I delete the data Karteria copied locally?
Settings → Delete local database. This does not delete the original data from Apple Health, only the copy Karteria uses for its charts.

How do I revoke Karteria's access to my Health data?
Apple Health → your profile → Apps and Devices → Karteria → turn off the data types you no longer want to share, or uninstall the app.
