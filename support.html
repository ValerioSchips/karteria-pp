Frequently asked questions

Does Karteria store my data anywhere outside my phone?
No. Karteria has no server or account: all data read from Apple Health stays in a local database on your device. The only copy outside your phone is your device's normal backup (iCloud or computer), if you have it turned on, managed by iOS. It reaches the developer only if you send a data export to support yourself. See the privacy policy for details.

Why doesn't Karteria show my runs?
Check that the app has permission to read data from Apple Health (Health → your profile → Apps → Karteria) and that your runs are recorded as running workouts in Apple Health (from Karteria's Watch companion, Apple's own Workout app, or another compatible app).

How do I delete the data Karteria copied locally?
Uninstall the app: this deletes the local database and everything else Karteria keeps on the device. Settings → "Full resync" deletes the local database too, but rebuilds it straight away from Apple Health. Neither touches the original data in Apple Health.

How do I revoke Karteria's access to my Health data?
Health app → your profile → Apps → Karteria → turn off the data types you no longer want to share, or uninstall the app.
